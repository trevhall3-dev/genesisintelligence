# Genesis Intelligence Hub — Collection and Editorial Policy
Effective: 2026-11-06

## Scope
Maintain a unified Genesis News feed. Collect official DOE Genesis Mission and national laboratory announcements and materially important DOE/laboratory AI and scientific intelligence developments. Do not add a separate DOE AI & SI navigation category.

## Verification (collector responsibility)
Research each item's Genesis relationship from official sources, mission/project documentation, and documented connections to already verified Genesis projects. Participation by a national laboratory is not sufficient evidence by itself. Record source links and rationale internally. Distinguish editorial relevance from verified mission affiliation.

## Display
- Confirmed Genesis connection: no visible affiliation badge.
- Relevant article lacking documented Genesis connection: display exactly **GENESIS LINK NOT CONFIRMED**.
- Never imply an unverified connection is verified.
- Reassess classification if later evidence emerges.

## Duplicate handling
Suppress only substantially identical article content, including minor headline rewrites or mirrors. Preserve distinct reporting, perspectives, technical detail, and follow-up milestones about the same subject. Compare article bodies rather than just title or project identity.

## Workflow
- Confirmed Genesis stories meeting editorial quality standards: eligible for automatic publication where an authorized publishing mechanism exists.
- Significant relevant stories with unconfirmed Genesis linkage: queue for publisher approval.
- Maintain internal metadata for affiliation status, source evidence, topical tags, review state, dates, and original URLs.
- Verify site/database writes before reporting publication.

## Publisher source classification (approved November 12, 2026)
News tiers use the **original article publisher** rather than a laboratory, company, or university merely mentioned in a headline. DOE-published pages belong to DOE; official national laboratory publisher sites belong to Labs; other university, industry, media, and independently published stories belong to Partners. These are mutually exclusive publisher labels, not claims of official Genesis partnership or affiliation. All News combines the three categories without repeating a URL. Apply the rules to archived news, fresh collection, D1 reads, and frontend filters. The separate private partnership candidate collector is paused (underlying records and code retained); the general third-party news collector continues.
